This paper presents Circle3™, a compact pattern language for leading change and building coherence in complex organizational environments. Organizational change initiatives often stall not because people do not care, but because they are operating from different interpretations of what is happening; priorities compete, conversations fragment, and decisions do not hold long enough to become practice. Circle3 offers Three Guiding Moves™—Establish the Circle, Integrate the Conversation, and Reconcile the Intention—that help groups form shared perception, work tension productively, and translate emerging clarity into durable commitments. Three Leadership Lenses™ in the appendix—Sense Making, Energy Awareness, and Paradigm Awareness—provide perceptual supports that help facilitators notice differences in interpretation, relational energy, and the paradigms shaping how people respond. Together, the Three Guiding Moves help groups turn shared perception into coordinated, durable action.

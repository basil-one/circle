This paper presents Circle3™, three guiding moves for leading difficult change by building shared understanding at the human layer. Change efforts often stall when agreement on plans, processes, and documentation is mistaken for shared understanding. People may pursue the same goals while holding different interpretations, intentions, and constraints. **Establish the Circle** creates a small, recurring space for people to reason together; **Integrate the Conversation** helps different perspectives and tensions become shared insight; and **Reconcile the Intention** carries that understanding into coordinated action through a visible representation of shared intention. The Moves can be revisited as circumstances change. Three Leadership Lenses™ in the appendix offer optional reflective exercises to support facilitation, particularly when conversations become difficult or stuck.

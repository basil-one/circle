## Conclusion

In difficult change, plans and formal agreements are not enough when people lack shared understanding; ambiguity can also leave them without a common direction. Circle3 offers three guiding moves for working with these challenges within the human layer of change. **Establish the Circle** creates recurring space for shared understanding, **Integrate the Conversation** helps people work through differences and tension, and **Reconcile the Intention** carries emerging understanding into coordinated action.

Coherence is not a final state achieved once. As conditions change, people can return to the conversation, integrate new perspectives, and renew their capacity to move together.

## Conclusion

Circle3 offers three guiding moves for leading difficult change at the human layer. **Establish the Circle** creates recurring space for shared understanding, **Integrate the Conversation** helps people work through differences and tension, and **Reconcile the Intention** carries that understanding into coordinated action.

Coherence is not a final state achieved once. As circumstances change, people can return to the conversation, integrate what they have learned, and move together again.

## Discussion

The Three Guiding Moves reinforce one another without forming a fixed sequence. **Establish the Circle** provides a recurring space where shared understanding can develop. **Integrate the Conversation** can also be used wherever people are working through differing perspectives and tensions. **Reconcile the Intention** helps people develop shared meaning around a visible intention and carry it into decisions and behavior.

Each Move may reveal a need for another. Reconciling an intention can surface differences that require integrative dialogue; integrating a conversation can reveal the need for a more sustained conversational space. As circumstances change, practitioners can revisit the Moves according to what the situation requires.

This continual adjustment happens within the **human layer of change**—where people interpret circumstances, work through differences, and coordinate their intentions. Formal structures serve other necessary functions: governance establishes authority and decision rights; program management coordinates work; organizational design defines roles; and technical practices address specialized problems. Circle3 complements those structures by helping people use them coherently.

Facilitation is an important capacity for applying the Moves. Establish the Circle calls for integrative facilitation to help different perspectives develop shared meaning; Integrate the Conversation makes this capacity especially explicit by working directly with attention, participation, tension, and conversational flow; and Reconcile the Intention depends on structured dialogue or collaborative review that can surface and reconcile differing interpretations. The Moves therefore assume sufficient facilitation capability is present, whether it resides in a dedicated facilitator or is shared among participants. Developing this capability is outside the scope of this paper.

Future work has two immediate directions. First, reflecting on continued use across organizational and community contexts can inform refinements to the Moves and Leadership Lenses, particularly their forces, failure modes, relationships, and boundaries. Second, because integrative facilitation is fundamental to the Moves, supporting patterns may be mined that make this capacity more explicit and distributed, allowing participants to take greater responsibility for maintaining coherent conversation together.
